# Insure

A comparison-first health insurance app for Cameroon. Insure lets people fill in their details once and see side-by-side quotes from multiple private health insurers, then buy and manage their policy in one place.

## Why

Cameroon's health insurance market is small, broker-dominated, and opaque — shopping around today means separate phone calls to each insurer. Insure's Couverture Santé Universelle (the state's free/subsidized care) covers only priority cases (under-5s, maternity, HIV/TB/dialysis), leaving working adults and families to buy private cover on their own, with no easy way to compare options.

## What v1 does

- Simple onboarding to capture the details insurers price on (age, family size, existing conditions, coverage tier)
- A dashboard comparing quotes from multiple insurers side by side
- A secure payment flow for buying a policy, built around Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Orange Money) with card as a secondary path
- An insurer-facing portal where companies enter their rate tables and underwriting rules, which Insure's backend uses to compute quotes
